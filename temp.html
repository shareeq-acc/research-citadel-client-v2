Features

Everything your research team needs.

Research Citadel is built for teams who take their work seriously — from solo researchers to multi-institution collaborations.

Research Vaults
Isolated workspaces per project. Set public or private, write abstracts, and control access with granular roles.
Citation Library
Upload PDFs or add web article URLs. Store full metadata — authors, journal, year, DOI — all organised and searchable.
AI Insights
Auto-extract research problems, methodology, key findings, contributions, limitations, and future work from every paper.
Grounded Q&A
Ask anything about your sources. Answers come only from your corpus — fully grounded, zero hallucinations, select per-query.
Live Annotations
Markdown notes mapped to exact pages and sections. Real-time collaboration with conflict detection and full version history.
Team Chat
Per-vault chat with threading, replies, typing indicators, and slash commands to reference sources — right where the research lives.
